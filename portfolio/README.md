## 3. Design Philosophy & Visual Palette

Designed specifically for **Accounting Firms, Finance Companies, Investment Banks, and Corporate Operations**:
- **Canvas:** Crisp off-white (`#fcfbfa` and `#ffffff`) for clarity and readability.
- **Typography:** Deep Corporate Navy (`#0a192f`, `#1e293b`) paired with `Playfair Display` (editorial serif) and `Plus Jakarta Sans` (corporate sans).
- **Accent:** Subtle Champagne Gold (`#c59b27`) for restrained executive elegance.
- **Dividers:** Clean 1px hairline borders (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Disciplines:** Zero pill badges on static metadata (text separated with `·` or `/`), single-level card elevation, WCAG AA compliance.
